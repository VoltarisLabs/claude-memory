# Title Voice Brand Tone

> The single source of truth for how Title Voice sounds. Every email, dashboard label, FAQ, landing page, video script, and client-facing document follows these rules.

**Last Updated:** February 16, 2026

---

## Core Principle

**Sell the outcome, not the mechanism.** The prospect cares that calls get answered and deals stop slipping. They do not care how the technology works under the hood.

---

## The Rules

### 1. Never say "AI" in customer-facing copy

The product is an autonomous receptionist, an intelligent system, an adaptive platform. Customers buy results, not acronyms.

| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| AI-powered | Autonomous, Intelligent, Adaptive |
| AI agent | Receptionist, System, Platform |
| AI assistant | Taylor, Intelligent receptionist, Dedicated line |
| Machine learning | Cognitive, Adaptive, Self-optimizing |
| Chatbot | Voice receptionist, Autonomous assistant |
| Automation | Orchestration, Agentic workflow |
| NLP / Natural Language | Neural processing, Conversational intelligence |
| Our product | The support, What we built for you |

**Internal docs** (database schemas, developer specs, API references) can reference the technology directly for clarity.

### 2. Discovery-based, not pushy

Open curiosity gaps that only close by engaging further. Let the prospect arrive at the conclusion themselves. The buyer must feel like they are making the decision.

Most title companies don't know they have a missed call problem. Our job is to make them aware of revenue they're losing that they don't know they're losing.

### 3. Loss aversion over gain framing

"Every missed call is a deal that walked" lands harder than "Answer more calls." Lead with what they are already losing.

Show them the fire they're standing in, then hand them the exit.

### 4. Grounded confidence

State facts. Show numbers. No superlatives, no hype, no "revolutionary" filler. Let the results speak.

### 5. No em dashes

Use periods, commas, or restructure the sentence. Every time.

### 6. No buzzwords

If a phrase sounds like it belongs in a pitch deck, rewrite it.

### 7. Short, direct sentences

If a sentence needs a second comma, it probably needs to become two sentences.

## Language Bible

These substitutions apply across all campaigns, UI copy, emails, and client-facing documents.

| Never Say | Always Say |
|-----------|-----------|
| "the price is $1,500" | "the investment is $1,500" |
| "if you want to move forward" | "when we get you started" |
| "I think this is what you need" | "this is the exact support you need" |
| "trust me" | "I'm here to help you" |
| "just following up" | "I had a few ideas I wanted to share" |
| "can I give you the pitch" | "can I share how we're going to help" |
| "fix the overflow problem" | "see the revenue you didn't know was missing" |
| "you're losing money" | "there's revenue going somewhere you can't see" |
| "get started" | "when we get you set up" |
| "sign up" | "lock in your spot" |
| "missed calls" | "calls that went somewhere else" / "calls that never left a trace" |
| "we answer your calls" | "every call gets answered and tracked" |
| "our product" | "the support" / "what we built for you" |

## Dashboard UI Copy

The dashboard is the product customers interact with daily. Write it like it matters.

| Element | How to Write It |
|---------|----------------|
| Empty state (no calls yet) | "No calls yet. Once Taylor starts answering, every call will show up here." |
| Live call indicator | "1 call happening right now" (not "1 active session") |
| Call summary | "Asked about closing date for 123 Main St" (not "Inquiry regarding property closing schedule") |
| Sentiment labels | "Positive" / "Neutral" / "Frustrated" (not "Positive" / "Neutral" / "Negative") |
| Stats cards | "Calls Handled" not "Total Calls Processed" |
| Recording label | "Play Recording" not "Audio Playback" |
| Transcript label | "Read Transcript" not "View Conversation Log" |
| Transfer context | "Transferred to John, caller needed help with title insurance quote" |
| Error state | "Something went wrong loading your calls. We're on it." (not "Error 500: Internal Server Error") |

| Use | Don't Use |
|-----|-----------|

| Answered | Serviced |

## Onboarding Copy

These rules apply to every header, subheader, label, helper text, email, and tooltip the client sees during onboarding.

| Rule | Do | Don't |
|------|-----|-------|
| Never say "AI" | "intelligent receptionist," "your dedicated line," "the system" | "AI agent," "AI-powered," "AI assistant" |
| Sell the outcome | "Every call gets answered and tracked" | "Our AI answers your calls" |
| Discovery over telling | Let them see the value themselves | Don't explain the mechanism |
| No em dashes | Use periods. Short sentences. | Long compound sentences |
| No hype or superlatives | "built for title companies" | "revolutionary AI solution" |
| Grounded confidence | "Here's what we need from you." | "Let's get started on your exciting journey!" |
| Investment, not price | "the investment" | "the price," "the cost" |
| Setup language | "when we get you set up" | "get started," "sign up" |

## Copy Examples

**Good:**
> Your phones ring 40 times a day. Your team answers maybe 25. The other 15 go to voicemail. Some of those callers try again. Most don't. They call the next company on their list.

**Bad:**
> Our revolutionary AI-powered solution leverages cutting-edge NLP to transform your customer engagement experience and drive unprecedented ROI.

**Good (FAQ):**
> The calls your team has been missing? They stop getting missed. The voicemails piling up at 5:01 PM? Gone. The agents who stopped referring you because nobody picked up? They start calling back.

**Bad (FAQ):**
> Title Voice is an AI-powered answering solution that uses natural language processing to handle inbound call volume and optimize your team's productivity.

## Who We Talk To

Operations directors, managing partners, and agency owners at title companies who are:
- Losing calls to voicemail and losing referral relationships because of it
- Paying for answering services that take messages but never resolve anything
- Scaling locations without wanting to scale headcount
- Competing against larger firms with deeper front-desk staffing

The copy should make them feel understood, not sold to.

## CTA Strategy

- **"Get Started"** links to the Pricing page
- **"Watch Demo"** opens an in-page video modal showing the product in action
- **"Schedule a Demo"** links to a live booking calendar for hands-on walkthrough
- No aggressive pop-ups, no countdown timers, no false urgency

## Voice Tone (Video / Audio)

When recording voiceovers or creating video content:

| Quality | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| **Speech rate** | Natural-fast pace. Don't slow down for emphasis. Faster signals confidence. |
| **Pitch** | Lower register. Don't uptalk. Low pitch signals authority. |
| **Intonation** | End every statement going DOWN, not up. Falling intonation signals authority. |
| **Pauses** | 1-second pause BEFORE important numbers, not after. |
| **Delivery** | Speak from the chest, not the throat. Confident, not performative. |

Never say anything as a question that should be a statement.

## Quick Checklist

Before publishing any customer-facing copy, check:

- [ ] Zero uses of "AI," "AI-powered," "AI agent," or "chatbot"
- [ ] Zero em dashes
- [ ] Zero superlatives ("revolutionary," "cutting-edge," "best-in-class")
- [ ] Zero buzzwords that sound like a pitch deck
- [ ] Leads with what they're losing, not what they'll gain
- [ ] Short sentences. No sentence has two commas.
- [ ] Uses "investment" not "price" or "cost"
- [ ] Uses "when we get you set up" not "get started" or "sign up"
- [ ] Specific numbers and real scenarios over vague claims
- [ ] The reader feels understood, not sold to
